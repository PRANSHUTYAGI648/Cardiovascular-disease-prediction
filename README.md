# Cardiovascular-disease-prediction
❤️ What is Cardiovascular Disease?

Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) means diseases or problems related to the heart and blood vessels.

Our heart pumps blood to the whole body. Blood vessels carry this blood to different parts of our body. When there is a problem with the heart or blood vessels, it is called cardiovascular disease.

For example:

Heart attack
Heart failure
High blood pressure
Problems in blood vessels
Coronary artery disease
🤖 What is our project?

Cardiovascular Disease Prediction is a Machine Learning project.

In this project, we use a person's health-related information, such as:

Age
Gender
Blood pressure
Cholesterol
Blood sugar
Chest pain
Heart rate
Other medical/health features

Our project is called Cardiovascular Disease Prediction.

First, let us understand what cardiovascular disease means. Cardiovascular disease is a group of diseases that affect the heart and blood vessels. When the heart or blood circulation system does not work properly, it can lead to serious health problems such as heart attack and other heart-related diseases.

Our project uses Machine Learning to predict the risk of cardiovascular disease.

We provide the model with health-related information such as age, gender, blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar, heart rate, and other important features.

The Machine Learning model learns patterns from existing patient data. After learning these patterns, it can analyze new patient information and predict whether the person is at risk of cardiovascular disease or not.

The main purpose of our project is to provide an early prediction and create awareness about heart-related health risks.

However, this prediction is only a supporting tool. It does not replace a doctor's diagnosis or medical advice.

In simple words, our project uses health data and Machine Learning to identify the possible risk of cardiovascular disease at an early stage.

The Machine Learning model studies the data and learns the patterns between these factors and cardiovascular disease.

Then, when we provide new patient data, the model can predict whether the person may be at risk of cardiovascular disease or not.
